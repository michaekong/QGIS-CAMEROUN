# About QGIS Cameroon

We are the official QGIS User Group for Cameroon, a professional community dedicated to promoting open-source geospatial technology, fostering capacity building, and supporting GIS practitioners across the nation.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to advance the adoption and effective use of open-source Geographic Information Systems in Cameroon by:
- Fostering collaboration and networking among GIS professionals, academics, and students.
- Promoting knowledge sharing, open data initiatives, and geospatial best practices.
- Supporting the integration of open-source GIS solutions in both public and private sectors.
- Contributing to the global QGIS community by sharing local use cases and innovations.
- Providing structured learning opportunities and mentorship for all skill levels.

## Our Community

We welcome individuals and institutions interested in geospatial technologies, from complete beginners to seasoned professionals. Our membership comprises:
- GIS professionals and certified cartographers
- environementalist and natural resource managers
- Urban planners and territorial development experts
- Academic researchers and university lecturers
- Geomatics students and interns
- Open-source enthusiasts and civic mappers

## Core Values

Our community operates on a foundation of strict professional and ethical standards:
- **Openness:** We champion open-source principles, transparent collaboration, and open data.
- **Inclusivity:** We provide a welcoming environment for all, regardless of background, gender, or technical expertise.
- **Continuous Learning:** We are committed to peer-to-peer mentorship and ongoing capacity building.
- **Active Contribution:** We encourage members to actively participate in projects and give back to the geospatial ecosystem.
- **Strict Neutrality:** We promote QGIS and free geospatial software without denigrating proprietary solutions, maintaining political, religious, and commercial neutrality.
